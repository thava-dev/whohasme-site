Privacy policy

WhoHasMe for iPhone and Mac · Draft, last updated 27 October 2026

The short version

WhoHasMe doesn't collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, so we never see your information.

What the app stores, on your device only

  • Your spaces, the email addresses, phone numbers and postal addresses you enter, and the places you link them to.
  • References to ID documents and payment cards: the type, issuing country or bank, and at most the last 4 characters. Never the full number.
  • Your notes and settings.

What the app never stores

Passwords, two-factor codes or secrets, backup codes, PINs, card security codes, full ID, card or account numbers, or scans of documents.

Contacts

If you allow it during setup, WhoHasMe reads only your own contact card (your “Me” card), once, to add your own details. It never reads your other contacts, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Backups

You can export a backup file. It is encrypted with a passphrase you choose, unless you explicitly turn that off. You decide where the file goes; we never receive it.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details or personal information.

Crash reports

We don't include any crash-reporting or analytics tools. If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us.

Children

WhoHasMe doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
