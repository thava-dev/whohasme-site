Do iPhone and Mac sync?

Not in this version. Move your map between them with Export and Import. Importing replaces what's on the other device, and the app makes an automatic backup first.

Can you recover my data?

No. Your map stays on your device and we never receive a copy, so keep a backup with Export. If you forget the passphrase of an encrypted backup, nobody can open that file.

Does it store passwords?

No, and it never will. Keep your password manager for those. For documents and cards, WhoHasMe keeps the type and at most the last 4 characters.

Does it find my data for me?

No. You record it yourself. WhoHasMe doesn't scan your email, browser or the web, and it doesn't remove your data from other places.

What does it cost?

Free for up to 25 places, then a one-time purchase for unlimited places. There's no subscription.
