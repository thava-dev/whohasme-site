See every place, and how it uses you

Pick an email or number and see the count: which places have it, in which role (login, recovery, 2FA, shipping), and in which space. You fill it in yourself, in seconds. Nothing is scanned.

Change numbers, skip lockouts

Retire an old number, email or address and pick its replacement. You get a checklist of every place to update, and the old one keeps its history, so you know who may still have it.

Keep your spaces apart

Personal, Work, Shopping, a client. When a work email ends up at a personal place, or an alias forwards to a mailbox in another space, you see it as an issue. Fix it, or accept it if it's on purpose.

A real Mac app, too

Native on both. iPhone and Mac don't sync in this version: move your map between them with Export and Import.

Nothing leaves your device

  • No account, no server, no analytics.
  • No networking code, and the Mac app has no permission to use the network.
  • Planned App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.
  • Locked with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode or password, and backups encrypted by default.
  • Never asks for passwords or 2FA codes. Cards and documents keep the last 4 characters at most.

Online and offline

Apps, banks and shops, and also your doctor, landlord and gym.

Works with your aliases

Any provider. See which alias went to which place.

Free for 25 places

Then $9.99 once for unlimited places. No subscription.

A map, not a vault.

Keep your password manager. WhoHasMe works alongside it.
